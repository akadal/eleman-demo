# Enron announcements, early 2000

- [1999-12-30] Credit Markets Group (announced 7 Dec by John Sherriff, Enron Europe): market-making in bankruptcy protection on credits in Enron's portfolio, traded on EnronOnline, plus an external web site with prices, models and research; London-based with a Houston desk; Bryan Seyfried leaves RAC to lead it; public announcement planned for late January. See [[research-requests-1999-q4]] for the white-paper asks.
- [1999-12-30] ENA reorganisation memo (16 Dec, Cliff Baxter and Kevin Hannon): midstream and downstream coverage groups consolidated under Dave Delainey; Greg Piper returns from Portland to run Downstream Origination: pulp and paper (Ed Ondarza), chemicals/plastics/refined products (Jim Ajello), non-integrated industrials (Bruce Garner, ex-Bankers Trust).
- [2000-01-05] Enron OnLine and Tracking meeting moved to Friday 7 Jan, 2:15-3:15 pm, EB 3084 (Alex Saldana); Vince had it put on his calendar.
- [2000-01-05→] Enterprise-wide Risk Management meeting Friday 21 Jan, 12-5 pm, DoubleTree (400 Dallas, Suite 407), with Rick Buy, Ted Murphy, Rick Carson, Dave Gorte, Kevin Kindall, Grant Masson and Vince; on his calendar.
- [2000-01-07→] Vince asked Shirley to set a regular Enron NA meeting on Mondays, 9:30-11:00.
