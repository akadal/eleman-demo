# Promotions, Jan 2000

- [2000-01-10] Vince's promotion to Managing Director was announced; Mark Lay, John Sherriff, Richard Shapiro and about ten others wrote to congratulate him, and he thanked them.
- [2000-01-10] [[kevin-moore]] asked to celebrate his boss [[mike-roberts]]'s promotion; Vince planned a celebration for next Thursday (20 Jan) and will talk to Kevin.
- [2000-01-10] Vince sent Greg Whalley the proposed bonuses for the research group's managers, directors and assistants (amounts omitted here); the VP numbers were already with Greg.
- [2000-01-11] After reading the memo on promotions Vince sent congratulations to about 25 promoted colleagues (among them Louise Kitchen, Janet Dietrich, Joe Gold, Richard Lewis, [[bryan-seyfried]], Dale Surbey, Anthony Dayao, Laura Luce, Rick Carson, Jeremy Blachman); replies from Rick Carson, Laura Luce, Anthony Dayao and Jeremy Blachman congratulated him back. Beth Perlman (London) wants the dinner club restarted; Vince will be in London in February.
- [2000-01-11] Bill Cordes wrote that he values the group's work for ET&S; [[clayton-vernon]] asked whether "Constrained Technical" resembles constrained optimization, and Vince answered that "constrained" means "from a tight labor pool".
- [2000-01-11] Stella Ely submitted a Payroll Reclassification Request for Vince's approval; the mail carries only a link, no details.
