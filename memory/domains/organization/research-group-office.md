# Research group office

- [1999-12-10] The research group moves to the 19th floor.
- [void: 2000-01-10] [1999-12-10] Vince's office is EB1933 (EB1928-EB1949): rooms were renumbered on move day; Vince is in EB1962, see [[research-group-seating-2000-01]].
- [1999-12-14] [[kevin-moore]] arranged a color printer for the 19th floor: HP 4500n at $2753 (cost center 100038), ordered via Enron Technology for Monday delivery; location EB1944 B.
- [1999-12-14] Kevin Moore asked for another Stentofon for Patricia Tlapek, who works closely with traders.
- [1999-12-13] The holiday party is postponed to after New Year (the Scout House was unavailable; Vince suggested waiting because of the move and vacations); the gift-name drawing was done. [[shirley-crenshaw]] announced it.
- [1999-12-17] Martina Angelova left the group for Enron Europe.
- [1999-12-21] The move to the 19th floor was reported complete.
- [2000-01-04] The postponed party became the "Millennium" party on Saturday 8 Jan at Stinson's house, 3421 Cason (address corrected 5 Jan); Vince had Greg and Liz invited. See [[research-group-equipment-2000-01]] and [[research-group-staffing-2000-01]] for later items.
