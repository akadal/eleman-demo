# Model risk audit, autumn 1999

Source: Vince's own sent mail, re-sent to his AOL address on 12-30; dates inside are event dates.

- [1999-12-30] Phase 2 of the review (request of 1 Sept from Derek Claybrook of the review team) asked what role the research group had in models not in its library: FPL, West/Central basis options, LCRA spread option, Welded Tube swaption, heating oil exotics (Lotus), NOX, interest rate, and deals Aruba, Oglethorpe, NPI (JEDI), Peakers, Bug, Snake River, structured credit.
- [1999-12-30] Vince's reply of 21 Sept: even when research builds a model it usually does not control it or its uses (spreadsheets can be modified by users); the group acts as internal consultants on narrow problems, and its involvement may later be read as unconditional endorsement.
- [1999-12-30] His comments of 3-4 Oct on the draft Model Development Report (Andrew Schuleman; Controller's meeting 1 Oct): model risk is very significant, since a wrong model leads to accounting errors. The main cause is that approval and validation are dispersed with no single owner, whereas the common belief is that research does it; add wrong inputs and wrong model for a deal (TVA: inputs not updated for long); mention the audits of research models by Arthur Andersen and Darrell Duffie, started at his insistence; the scope must include Risk Analytics and IT or the conclusions will be inaccurate.
