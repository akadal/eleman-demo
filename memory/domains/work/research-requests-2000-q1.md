# Research requests, early 2000

- [1999-12-26→] Research Intelligence: Vince asked [[stinson-gibner]] to review a memo and send it on to Ross Prevatt; binders of the newsletter's Technical Corner articles go to senior Enron executives (Skilling, Sutton, Hannon, Baxter, Delainey, Buy, Whalley, Shankman and others), each asked to say where the techniques could help.
- [1999-12-27] Stinson sent Wes Colwell a spreadsheet valuing the RTHM put position (subject "LJM put valuation").
- [2000-01-04→] Amerada Hess day-rate hedge: [[john-peyton]] sent history for 4th/5th-generation semi-submersible rates; the rig rate is $155,000/day for four years; start with a $100,000 floor for 1 Jan 2001 - 31 Dec 2004 and find the costless call strike. Rest of the data (Sept-Dec 99) came 7 Jan; analysis expected Tuesday 11 Jan.
- [2000-01-04] Gary Taylor (weather) is structuring a hurricane-linked interest rate swap and asked Vince for the effect of a category 5 hurricane hitting Miami on long and short rates; Vince said it would bankrupt a few insurers and went by in the afternoon.
- [2000-01-05→] Argentina modelling: [[michael-guerriero]] (runs Enron's Argentina operation) asked for a meeting; Vince's Thursday and Friday slots were taken; Vince told him to call Shirley (3-5290) to book another day.
- [2000-01-06] Clayton asked about Russian loan guarantees; Vince: ExIm guarantees bank and commercial transactions (commercial risk), OPIC insures US-owned physical assets abroad against political risk; Brad Blesie would know more.
- [2000-01-07→] Boris Brevnov (Enron Communications) sent a strategic plan for Vince's comments.
- [2000-01-07] [[pinnamaneni-krishnarao]] travels to India: Houston 9 Jan, Bombay (with [[sandeep-kohli]]) until 14th, Hyderabad 15-16, vacation 17-18 in Vijayawada, back in Houston Thursday 20 Jan.
