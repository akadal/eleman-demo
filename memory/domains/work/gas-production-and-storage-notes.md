# Gas production and storage notes

- [1999-12-27] [[clayton-vernon]] spoke with Roy Kass of the Energy Information Administration: published state-level wellhead production estimates arrive late; EIA scientists see weather in the fields (freezes, hurricanes) as a far bigger driver of production and wellhead prices than Northeast weather; Kass agreed with Vince that marginal cost of production is strictly increasing, with wells varying in efficiency, technology, maintenance and investment. Vince sent it on to his AOL address on 12-30.
- [1999-12-30] CERA call of 14 Dec, forwarded: warm weather eased gas prices for now but fundamentals point to tighter markets; summer injections may strain supply; Northeast volatility continues with or without new pipeline capacity.
- [2000-01-06] Clayton, from the class: pipelines are adopting operational balancing agreements that absorb all production and consumption imbalances, so shippers book the schedule; producing-region storage protects pipeline integrity, so estimated marketed production is probably a better estimate of flow at the receipt meter than production less local storage.
- [2000-01-07] Vince forwarded ESAI's "Natural Gas Fundwatch" issue, "Bearish Pressure on Gas Price Continues".
